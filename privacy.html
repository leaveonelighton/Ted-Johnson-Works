Privacy Policy

Effective date: Upon publication · Last updated: November 21, 2026

The short version: When you contact Ted Johnson Works, we use the details you provide to respond and provide the information or service you requested. An Amazon referral request does not sign you up for a mailing list. Please do not email sensitive documents, passwords, Social Security numbers, or payment-card information.

1. Who runs this website

Ted Johnson Works is an assumed name of The De Facto Meatball King L.L.C. in Texas. This policy covers tedjohnsonworks.com and communications made directly with Ted Johnson Works. Leave One Light On is a separate organization and is not covered by this policy.

Privacy questions and requests: ted@tedjohnsonworks.com.

2. Information you choose to provide

  • Emails: When you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you include.
  • Amazon employee referral requests: Emails sent to referrals@tedjohnsonworks.com are handled by our email provider, Hostinger, to send an automatic response with referral instructions. The request does not create an account on this website. The auto-response may be limited to one reply per sender within 24 hours.
  • Technology Checkup form: The Small Business form can collect a business name, your name, a description of your challenge, email address (required), phone, preferred contact method, website, and city/state. Its server-side handler uses Hostinger email services to forward the submission to Ted Johnson Works for follow-up.
  • Other services: If you request a consultation, buy a guide or photograph, or pay for services, the provider involved may request additional information necessary to process that transaction.

3. Information created when you visit

As with most websites, hosting providers may process technical information such as an IP address, browser details, timestamps, and requested pages to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the site. The exact server-log retention period and any analytics or cookie settings are under review; we will update this notice if those details change or are confirmed.

4. Why we use information

We use information to respond to messages, provide requested referral instructions, evaluate service inquiries, arrange and deliver requested work, maintain business records when needed, and help keep the website and email service secure. The referral mailbox is not intended as a mailing-list signup. We do not use referral requests as permission to send unrelated marketing emails.

5. Who processes information

Hostinger provides website hosting and email services. Other independent services may handle information when you choose to use them, including payment and sales providers such as Stripe, Payhip, and Shopify. An Amazon application takes place on Amazon's hiring website, subject to Amazon's own privacy practices and decisions. Links to third-party services take you outside TJW; review their privacy notices before providing personal or payment information.

We do not offer lists of referral-request email addresses for sale. Information may be disclosed if required by law or to operate, secure, and provide a service you request.

6. How long information is kept

Email messages and service inquiries are kept only as long as reasonably needed for responding, providing services, resolving issues, maintaining necessary business records, or meeting legal obligations. A fixed deletion schedule has not yet been established for the Hostinger mailboxes and website server logs. Until it is verified and implemented, we cannot promise automatic deletion after a particular number of days. You can ask us to delete an inquiry, subject to records we need or are legally required to retain.

7. Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you provided directly to TJW by emailing ted@tedjohnsonworks.com. We may need to confirm that a request concerns your own information. We will respond and explain any legal or practical limits on deletion. You do not need to send personal documents to request Amazon referral information.

8. Security and children

We use established hosting and service providers and take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary collection. No website or email system can guarantee complete security. Please do not send passwords, Social Security numbers, banking details, medical records, or payment-card numbers through our forms or email. The website is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Updates

We may revise this policy as our services or information practices change. The revised version will appear on this page with an updated date. This policy describes Ted Johnson Works; it does not replace the privacy policies of Amazon, Shopify, Stripe, Payhip, Hostinger, or other providers.
