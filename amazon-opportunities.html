An inside glimpse, not an inside track

I'm happy to share general, firsthand observations about returning to work, adapting to a different role, and practical questions worth asking before accepting a position. I can't speak for Amazon, access an applicant's hiring status, or influence hiring decisions.

  • Read the position's physical requirements and schedule carefully.
  • Consider your transportation, health, and the hours you can reliably work.
  • Use Amazon's official application website rather than paying anyone to apply.
  • Review how employment may affect retirement benefits, if applicable.
