LIFE IN PICTURES

Original photographs. Short stories. One life, still unfolding.

The album is designed to grow one chapter at a time. The originals stay the evidence. Cropping, contrast correction, dust cleanup, or privacy blurring may be used when needed, but historical scenes will not be replaced with AI-generated lookalikes.

01 Original archive photograph International test-team and workstation photographs selected from the personal archive.

CONTRACT TESTING YEARS

Testing a world I could not read

International software testing taught me to separate what I knew from what I assumed. When I could not read a language, I used the English version as a behavioral reference, watched structure and navigation, and learned that interface conventions can change across languages and reading directions.

02 Original archive photograph Family-in-the-lab photographs selected from the personal archive. Public captions will stay personal and historical rather than identifying the workplace unnecessarily.

FAMILY IN THE WORKSPACE

When my work world and family world crossed

Some of my favorite photographs are not polished corporate pictures. They are family memories made around CRT monitors, cables, equipment racks, temporary workstations, and the kind of makeshift test space that immediately dates the era.

03 Original archive photograph Group photograph selected. Coworkers will remain unnamed unless there is a good reason and permission to do otherwise.

THE TEAM

A photograph everybody got to take home

The old test-team photograph belongs here as memory, not as an endorsement. It records a group of people doing the work together during a very different technology era. The picture was distributed to the team, and my copy became part of my personal archive.

04 Original archive photograph Four Chinook photographs are in the archive and ready for the visual pass.

UNEXPECTED MOMENTS

Then a Chinook shows up

The archive also has the things no résumé would ever capture: a CH-47 Chinook on the grass, in the air, and close enough to turn an ordinary workday into a story worth keeping.

05 Original archive photograph The passenger-seat Starbucks photograph is not in the current archive bundle. It gets added when it turns up — no substitute image and no fake reconstruction.

THE SMALL DETAILS

A coffee cup can date a whole era

The little details can be as useful as the big milestones. A coffee cup in a passenger seat, an old monitor, a badge, a desk, a cable bundle, or a building can instantly bring back what everyday life felt like before the world changed around it.

Freedom 250: the next chapter, photographed in the present.

The historic album looks backward. Freedom 250 can look outward now: everyday people, work, roads, towns, tools, gatherings, machines, landscapes, and the freedom to build a life.

That keeps the 250th material connected to the same idea without turning the main site into a patriotic theme park: real life, real photographs, short stories, and useful context.
