# Ted Johnson Works Service Standard

## Small Business Technology Checkup

- Intake submission does not charge the customer or reserve a paid appointment.
- Ted reviews new requests within 2 business days.
- Capacity is confirmed before payment is requested.
- Launch capacity: no more than 5 paid Checkups per week and no more than 5 active paid Checkups in the delivery queue at once.
- If capacity is full, qualified prospects can be waitlisted rather than charged.
- After payment, a Checkup appointment is normally offered within 5 business days.
- The Checkup is approximately 60–90 minutes.
- The written Fix Now / Improve Next / Nice to Have action plan is normally delivered within 2 business days after the Checkup.
- One brief clarification follow-up is included for 7 days after delivery.
- Implementation is separate and requires its own scope and price.
- The Checkup is not 24/7 or emergency IT support.
- If TJW cannot meet a promised paid-service window, contact the client promptly and offer a revised mutually agreed date or a full refund.

Operating principle: Capacity before cash. Under-promise. Over-deliver.
