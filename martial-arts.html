MARTIAL ARTS · BOOK ON THE RADAR

Vo Lam — Original Vietnam Kung Fu

Not an expert’s encyclopedia. A regular practitioner’s book about unusually useful fighting lessons he was fortunate enough to receive — and how some of those ideas can complement the martial art a reader is already studying.

WORKING POSITION

Learn alongside the martial art you already train.

The concept does not require a reader to abandon their current system, memorize every Vo Lam form, or become a weapons specialist. It focuses on selected practical lessons that can sit beside the stance, kicking, blocking, movement, timing, and controlled technique work they are already learning.

WORKING SUBTITLE

Learn Alongside the Martial Art You’re Already Training

The premise is strong because the reader is already learning stance, movement, kicks, blocks, distance, timing, and control. Vo Lam can add another lens instead of asking them to start over.

Content radar: the traditional set of seventy-two techniques, elbow-strike material, practical applications, surviving equipment, personal memories, and enough historical context to explain where the lessons came from. Specific technique instruction should be presented as controlled martial-arts training and safety-focused practice, not as injury optimization.
