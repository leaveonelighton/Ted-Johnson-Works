SMALL BUSINESS TECHNOLOGY CHECKUP

Ten questions. Then the top three priorities.

No 40-page disaster report. No forced AI. No surprise changes.

  1. Can customers find the business easily and see accurate hours, phone, address, and website?
  2. Does the website make sense on a phone?
  3. Can customers quickly understand what the business offers and what to do next?
  4. Are calls, forms, booking, ordering, and payment paths working without unnecessary friction?
  5. Do Google, social profiles, website, logo, photos, and messaging look like the same business?
  6. Are important accounts protected and ownership of domains, websites, and key logins understood?
  7. Could the business recover important files, contacts, and website access after a failure?
  8. Is the business paying for overlapping or unused technology?
  9. Are employees repeating work that software or automation could simplify?
  10. Is AI being used deliberately, ignored completely, or purchased without a clear need?
