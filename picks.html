Ted Uses RV Cooling · Amazon

Furrion Chill Cube 18K RV AC

I upgraded my RV cooling because Texas heat does not care about excuses. The Chill Cube’s variable-speed design, lower noise, and extreme-heat performance earned its place here.

My field test
In 100°F-plus Texas heat, I measured about 55°F at the vent with the same handheld thermometer that showed about 69°F on my older unit. That is my own observation, not a lab test.
Why it matters
Variable-speed cooling can maintain comfort at lower output instead of always running flat-out, which can help with noise and energy use.
Know before you buy
My RV uses a ducted setup. The rooftop unit and ducted distribution/control components can be separate purchases, so verify your configuration first.
